Section 1

General Deep-Sky References

Comprehensive multi-object guides covering thousands of targets across the sky. The backbone of any serious observing library.

Night Sky Observer's Guide★
Volumes 1–4
George Robert Kepple & Glen W. Sanner et al.
Willmann-Bell · 1998–2017 Limited
An observing guide I reach for when planning most sessions. Volume 3 covers the southern sky. Many of my own visual descriptions appear throughout.
Annals of the Deep Sky★
A Survey of Galactic and Extragalactic Objects · Vol. 1–12
Jeff Kanipe & Dennis Webb
Willmann-Bell · 2014–ongoing Limited
An ongoing constellation-by-constellation survey blending astrophysics, history, and visual observation. Many of my visual descriptions appear throughout this series.
Observing Handbook and Catalogue of Deep-Sky Objects★
Christian B. Luginbuhl & Brian A. Skiff
Cambridge University Press · 1989 (reprinted) In print
Meticulous visual descriptions for over 2,000 objects observed with telescopes from 60mm to 305mm. A foundational reference and one I still consult constantly.
Interstellarum Deep Sky Guide★
Ronald Stoyan & Uwe Glahn
Oculum-Verlag · 2014 In print
A spiral-bound, field-ready guide with detailed labeled images and sketches for thousands of deep-sky objects. Designed to live on your observing table.
